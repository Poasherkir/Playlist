How it works

  1. Copy the playlist link

    Straight from your browser's address bar. The usual watch?v=...&list=... link works, and you get the whole playlist, not just the one video.

  2. Double-click the file you just downloaded

    The downloader opens in your browser. The first run takes a few minutes while it fetches what it needs; after that it opens straight away.

  3. Paste the link

    It starts there and then, with nothing to confirm. Watch the progress, then click through to the folder. Stop whenever you like. Next time it picks up where it left off.

Worth knowing

It runs on your computer, not on this site
YouTube blocks downloads coming from data centres, and a server has neither the disk nor the time for a playlist of videos. Running it on your own machine sidesteps both, and your files never touch anyone else's computer.
Nothing is installed
The tools it needs land in a bin folder next to the script. Nothing touches your PATH or your registry. Delete the folder and it is gone without trace.
It picks up where it stopped
Close it mid-playlist, come back tomorrow, choose the same folder, and it skips what you already have. Videos that fail get retried next run.
Files stay in order
Named 001 - Title.mp4 and so on, so your player keeps the playlist order. MP3s carry the thumbnail as cover art and proper titles.
